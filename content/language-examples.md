## Reading the examples

These 98 authored examples belong to the current Korvar reference. They illustrate the declared grammar; they do not claim native-speaker validation.

### C01: I am a dwarf.

**Ek sere Hamarkor.**

identity: Hamarkor is a predicate noun; no occupational implication.

### C02: We are dwarves.

**Vir sere Hamarkorar.**

number: Plural predicate noun; verb does not agree.

### C03: The stoneworker builds the chamber.

**Da menir karne da kel.**

main_v2: Subject + finite + object.

### C04: Now I build the chamber.

**Nu karne ek da kel.**

inversion: Time constituent + finite + subject.

### C05: The stoneworker speaks inside the chamber.

**Na da kelum vare da menir.**

preposition: Whole location phrase occupies first field.

### C06: Do you build the chamber?

**Karne du da kel?**

yes_no: Finite precedes subject.

### C07: Who builds the chamber?

**Hve karne da kel?**

subject_question: Interrogative is the subject.

### C08: What do you build?

**Hvat karne du?**

object_question: Object question retains explicit subject.

### C09: Where is the child?

**Hor sere da ner?**

where_question: Copula is finite second constituent.

### C10: With whom do you speak?

**Me hveum vare du?**

oblique_question: Interrogative takes oblique after me.

### C11: I do not build the chamber.

**Ek karne ne da kel.**

negation: Ne follows subject and finite.

### C12: Now I have not built the chamber.

**Nu have ek ne karnet da kel.**

negated_perfect: Inversion; negation before nonfinite participle.

### C13: I say that they do not build the chamber.

**Ek vare ke sa ne karne da kel.**

subordination: Singular sa; dependent negation before finite.

### C14: I know whether you build the chamber.

**Ek vite se du karne da kel.**

embedded_yes_no: No inversion in dependent question.

### C15: I know where you build the chamber.

**Ek vite hor du karne da kel.**

embedded_wh: Question word + subject + finite.

### C16: The person who builds the chamber is here.

**Da kor ri karne da kel sere her.**

subject_relative: Relative subject gap; entire noun phrase precedes main copula.

### C17: The chamber that I build is large.

**Da kel ri ek karne sere storr.**

object_relative: Relative object gap; predicate adjective remains uninflected.

### C18: The person with whom I speak is here.

**Da kor ri ek vare me saum sere her.**

oblique_relative: Resumptive pronoun saum after me.

### C19: The person who does not build the chamber is here.

**Da kor ri ne karne da kel sere her.**

relative_negation: Subject gap followed by ne + finite.

### C20: My chamber is here.

**Eks kel sere her.**

possessive_pronoun: Possessor replaces head determiner.

### C21: The stoneworker's chamber is here.

**Da menirs kel sere her.**

genitive_scope: Da belongs inside the possessor.

### C22: I have a chamber belonging to the stoneworker.

**Ek have kel af da menirum.**

indefinite_possession: Bare head plus postposed association phrase.

### C23: I give the stone to you.

**Ek give da men til duum.**

recipient: No bare double-object pattern.

### C24: They wash themself.

**Sa vaske si.**

reflexive: Singular reflexive refers to clause subject.

### C25: They wash another person referred to as they.

**Sa vaske sa.**

nonreflexive: Distinct third-person referent; repeat names if unclear.

### C26: They have their own hand hammer.

**Sa have sis handhamar.**

reflexive_possession: Sis is subject-bound.

### C27: They have another person's hand hammer.

**Sa have sas handhamar.**

external_possession: Sas is not subject-bound here.

### C28: We help one another.

**Vir helpe vir mut.**

reciprocal: Plural object plus reciprocal marker.

### C29: I speak with myself and you.

**Ek vare me sium ak duum.**

coordination: Reflexive sium is clause-subject-bound; both conjuncts carry oblique.

### C30: The stone is scratch-resistant and brittle.

**Da men sere darn ak spröd.**

properties: Hardness does not entail toughness.

### C31: This stone is harder than that stone.

**Di men sere mer darn dan dan men.**

comparison: First dan is comparison linker; second is determiner.

### C32: This chamber is as large as that chamber.

**Di kel sere sam storr as dan kel.**

equative: Sam + adjective + as introduces an equal comparison.

### C33: The stoneworker built the nursery.

**Da menir karnte da nerkel.**

past: Transparent past suffix -te.

### C34: I have built the chamber.

**Ek have karnet da kel.**

perfect: Have + participle.

### C35: I had built the chamber.

**Ek havte karnet da kel.**

pluperfect: Past auxiliary + participle.

### C36: I will build the chamber.

**Ek fere karnen da kel.**

future: Finite prospective auxiliary + infinitive.

### C37: I will have built the chamber.

**Ek fere haven karnet da kel.**

future_perfect: Future + infinitive perfect auxiliary + participle.

### C38: I can carry the stone.

**Ek mage bären da men.**

ability: Magen expresses ability.

### C39: I must remain silent.

**Ek mole stilen.**

obligation: Positive obligation with lexical silent verb.

### C40: I am not obliged to speak.

**Ek mole ne varen.**

modal_negation: Negation takes scope over obligation.

### C41: I prohibit speaking.

**Ek banne varen.**

prohibition: Activity infinitive; not I must not speak.

### C42: I permit the child to eat.

**Ek love ke da ner ete.**

permission: Explicit subordinate subject.

### C43: The chamber is being built by the stoneworker.

**Da kel vere karnet af da menirum.**

passive: Event passive plus agent phrase.

### C44: The chamber stands built.

**Da kel sere karnet.**

result_state: Copula plus result participle.

### C45: I am now engaged in building.

**Ek sere nu na karnadum.**

ongoing: Activity noun after na.

### C46: If the water flow stops, the wheel stops.

**Vel da drav stane, stane da rad.**

condition: Dependent clause fills first main-clause field.

### C47: If I had the stone, I would build the chamber.

**Vel ek vu havte da men, fere ek vu karnen da kel.**

counterfactual: Vu has documented dependent and main positions.

### C48: I come to help.

**Ek kome te helpen.**

purpose_shared: Infinitive shares main subject.

### C49: I build the chamber so that the child can sleep.

**Ek karne da kel te ke da ner mage soven.**

purpose_different: Te ke introduces explicit new subject.

### C50: As for the chamber, I build it.

**Tem da kel, ek karne di.**

topic: Outside-clause topic plus resumptive demonstrative.

### C51: Do not open the door.

**Ne op da dor.**

negative_imperative: Ne precedes bare imperative.

### C52: Please give the stone to me.

**Bitte, giv da men til ekum.**

polite_request: Imperative plus recipient in oblique.

### C53: Pump the water into the cistern.

**Pom da vas in da dravkelum.**

industrial_direction: Imperative and directional preposition.

### C54: The water flow powers the wheel.

**Da drav dreve da rad.**

industrial_power: Direct mechanical power relationship.

### C55: I test the sample.

**Ek tröve da trov.**

test_sample: Tröv and trov remain different words.

### C56: I label the sample with a mark.

**Ek merke da trov me markum.**

instrument: Me governs oblique.

### C57: The support-stone grade is not known.

**Da gred af stalmenum sere ne vitet.**

uncertainty: Explicit lack of knowledge; not a failed grade.

### C58: The sample is dry, by observation.

**Da trov sere tör, vis.**

evidence: Particle states the claimed basis.

### C59: I think the sample is dry.

**Ek tönke ke da trov sere tör.**

reported_belief: Thought complement rather than asserted measurement.

### C60: The egg is inside the egg compartment.

**Da egg sere na da eggkelum.**

reproduction: Egg distinct from compartment.

### C61: The child is in the nursery.

**Da ner sere na da nerkelum.**

care_location: No claim that the child is itself a geode.

### C62: I care for and protect the child.

**Ek ruke da ner.**

care_action: Protective caregiver role.

### C63: I feed the child with nourishment.

**Ek sele da ner me selum.**

feeding: Recipient is direct object; food is me phrase.

### C64: The child holds the hand hammer.

**Da ner halte da handhamar.**

body_action: No unselected digit count.

### C65: I want water.

**Ek vele vas.**

need: Bare mass noun.

### C66: I eat the tuber.

**Ek ete da röt.**

food: Food object of eten; functional crop analog rather than an Earth species.

### C67: I like the fungal food.

**Ek like da sombsel.**

preference: Enjoyment outside industrial register.

### C68: I love you.

**Ek elske du.**

affection: Direct object pronoun.

### C69: I grieve.

**Ek sorge.**

emotion: Intransitive feeling verb.

### C70: The child plays.

**Da ner spele.**

play: No object required.

### C71: We sing the song.

**Vir sange da sang.**

music: Related verb and noun stems.

### C72: I have no stone.

**Ek have neng men.**

negative_quantity: Neng already makes a negative quantity.

### C73: Two chambers are here.

**Tor kelar sere her.**

count: Numeral selects plural count noun.

### C74: I take two vessel-units of water.

**Ek take tor kapar af vasum.**

measure: Capacity unit and mass-noun complement.

### C75: The chamber is high and deeply covered.

**Da kel sere höl ak dypp.**

altitude_cover: Two independent spatial properties with contextual datums.

### C76: I travel sunward.

**Ek gare solvard.**

direction: Adverb, not preposition requiring a case.

### C77: I travel through the tunnel.

**Ek gare dor da gangum.**

route: Through-preposition distinct from door noun.

### C78: Do you know whether the water flows?

**Vite du se da vas drave?**

nested_question: Main inversion; dependent subject-first order.

### C79: I want to learn Common Dwarven.

**Ek vele leren Korvar.**

learning: Shared-subject infinitive.

### C80: I teach Common Dwarven to the child.

**Ek läre Korvar til da nerum.**

teaching: Content object; learner as explicit recipient.

### C81: Hello. Can you help me?

**Hei. Mage du helpen ek?**

greeting: Modal + infinitive + direct object.

### C82: Yes. What do you want?

**Ja. Hvat vele du?**

response: Yes response distinct from clause negation.

### C83: I want two dressed stone pieces.

**Ek vele tor menekar.**

trade_request: Bounded result noun, counted plural.

### C84: I give these to you.

**Ek give di til duum.**

demonstrative: Di resumes the previously requested plural stones.

### C85: Thanks. I will pay.

**Takk. Ek fere betalen.**

trade_close: Payment amount is contextually recoverable.

### C86: Farewell.

**Farvel.**

farewell: Conventional social formula.
