# Korvar and the language family


## The shared language


Dverghamar is the broad name used for the indigenous dwarven peoples and for their language family. Its regional languages share much of their grammar but differ in vocabulary and pronunciation. Korvar, literally people’s or public speech, is the standard taught for communication between regions. Hamarvar is the language of the ridge networks.


## Basic roots


| Root | Common reference meaning |
| --- | --- |
| hamar | Crag or great rock mass; related inherited hammer sense |
| rak | Natural rock material or an unworked rock piece |
| kam | Continuous bedrock in place |
| men | Stone selected as shaping material |
| kor | Person |
| var | Speech or language |


The last noun in a compound tells you what kind of thing it names. Earlier roots can describe its material, purpose, contents or mechanism; the dictionary records which meaning a compound has.


## A first example


The guides below cover pronunciation, grammar, the Stavmark script and five regional languages. The dictionary contains 562 lexical records, and the example collection has 97 translated sentences. You can begin with these two:


**Ek sere kor.**


I am a person.


**Vir sere korar.**


We are people.


Plural **-ar** attaches to ordinary count nouns, so **kor** 'person' gives **korar** 'people'. **Dverghamar** is an inherited collective proper name rather than the regular plural of a modern singular. **Hamarkar**, the planet-name, belongs to an older naming layer and should not be analyzed from modern **kor**.
