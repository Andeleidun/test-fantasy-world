Public guide
Worlds, peoples and languages of Andel’s Song


This collection contains 37 Markdown documents, this index and six data files. Its natural histories use comparisons within the reach of nineteenth-century scholarship. Spiritual accounts describe ordinary trained practitioners’ experiences; Otherworld accounts record human perceptions and stories. Observations, theories and religious beliefs are identified as such. Detailed technical models belong to the authorial collection, including models published in earlier editions.
All original project IP remains the author’s. “Public” grants no license, reuse permission, or public-domain dedication.
