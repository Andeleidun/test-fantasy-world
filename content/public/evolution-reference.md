# Native dwarven evolutionary history

## A native history

The Hamarkorar are one late branch of Dverghamar’s native biosphere. Their history begins with the planet’s water, rock and early microbial communities, long before the arrival of the Thals. The sequence below is the preferred evolutionary reconstruction. Its broad connections are more developed than its exact dates or fossil forms.

## Early waters and microbial worlds

Planet formation is followed by cooling, differentiation and the establishment of persistent water-bearing environments. Early life occupies chemically useful interfaces among water, minerals and dissolved gases. The location and mechanism of life’s origin are not specified.

Photosynthetic lineages later expand the available energy supply. Over geological time, biological production and mineral reactions alter atmospheric and aquatic chemistry. Oxygen becomes available in some environments before it can support a widespread oxygen-dependent animal biosphere.

These changes require sustained planetary cycling. Volcanism, weathering, burial and exposure repeatedly create new chemical opportunities. Early stellar activity and water retention are essential parts of this history, rather than problems that later organisms can solve retrospectively.

## Early large life

Simple multicellular organisms diversify in productive water. Attachment, grazing, filtering, scavenging and predation provide different routes to larger bodies. Oxygen availability and organic production limit where active consumers can thrive.

The ancestry leading to dwarves develops **three pairs of appendages** in water. Their early roles can include propulsion, holding position and manipulating food. The exact appearance of these animals remains open, but the paired structures precede the terrestrial body plan.

This lineage is one branch of the fauna. Other animals may have different limb arrangements, and descendants can reduce or lose structures over time. Dwarven ancestry does not require every large organism on the planet to have six limbs.

## Moving onto land

Shallow-water and marginal habitats favor stronger support, air breathing, moisture retention and controlled movement between pools or wet surfaces. Six appendages become capable of bearing weight before the lineage divides their tasks more sharply.

The twilight landscape supplies a patchwork of productive slopes, wetlands, rocky refuges and dry gaps. Movement through this terrain favors secure purchase and flexible handling. The hind pair increasingly provides propulsion and support; the front and middle pairs take on more manipulation and bracing.

A terrestrial egg and increasingly attentive parental care allow young to develop away from open water. Mineral-lined shelters can arise incrementally from protected nests, selected fragments and reinforced casings. The precise sequence joining these behaviors to the later mineral nursery is still a reconstruction rather than a described fossil record.

## Active mountain ancestors

Later animals combine sustained activity, improved temperature regulation and more complex social behavior. An endothermic ancestral branch is provisionally placed around **80–200 million years ago**. This broad interval indicates that the underlying physiology is much older than the dwarven genus.

Mountain and rocky-margin life rewards climbing, carrying and manipulation. Supporting the trunk while freeing two limb pairs requires changes to the skeleton, musculature, balance and breathing. The resulting lineage has two arm girdles and a separate weight-bearing hind-limb system.

Habitual upright postures become more important around **12–6 million years ago** in the current reconstruction. Habitual bipedal movement and increasingly consequential tool use follow by roughly **3–4 million years ago**. These activities develop together with hands, perception, social learning and parental dependence, rather than appearing in a single leap.

## The dwarven genus

The native genus is provisionally placed around **2.5–3.5 million years ago**. Four arms make simultaneous holding, support and manipulation useful in many tasks. Strength and endurance remain bounded by body size, energy supply, leverage and higher gravity.

Species divergence occurs through changing connections among ecological provinces. Mountains, arid basins, waterways and periods of isolation reduce exchange without making every boundary permanent. Later contact produces overlapping ranges and local exchange where biologically possible.

| Branch | Main direction of specialization |
| --- | --- |
| Central massif | A broadly capable mountain-interior lineage |
| Warm branch | Sunward dry-heat and volcanic-range species |
| Cold branch | Cold-edge and high-mountain species |
| Deep-massif population | A more enclosed-habitat subspecies within the central massif species |

The living species are provisionally separated over roughly **0.5–2 million years**, with the deep-massif subspecies developing over about **150,000–400,000 years**. These are approximate scenario intervals, not measured genetic divergence dates.

## Heat, cold and altitude

Sunward specialization favors water conservation and activity patterns suited to dry heat. Volcanic-range specialization faces a different combination of humid heat, ash, gases and locally distinctive minerals. The two settings need not produce identical physiology.

Cold-edge populations remain connected to food ultimately produced in the lit region. High-mountain populations face cold together with lower oxygen availability. Cold tolerance, acclimatization and oxygen transport offer several possible pathways, with tradeoffs in energy use and performance elsewhere.

The deep-massif subspecies retains fertile exchange with central massif populations. Its history relies on connections to shallower, food-bearing habitats and aquifers. It is not the product of a large, wholly isolated, naturally productive deep-rock world.

## From shelters to mineral nurseries

The preferred reproductive endpoint is a true egg incubated within a parent-built porous mineral enclosure, followed by a dependent hatchling. Natural selection can favor better site choice, mineral handling, pore maintenance and parental attention before later cultures formalize those practices.

Connected pores serve breathing; honeycombed supports serve structure. The same opening cannot be assumed to solve moisture retention, filtration and load-bearing equally well. Food comes from biological reserves and later provisioning, while selected minerals provide construction material and potentially specific chemical functions.

Dwarven tolerance of local substances can differ substantially from human tolerance after long adaptation. Learned identification and shared material experience add another layer of protection. Exact egg dimensions, oxygen fluxes, incubation times and feeding structures remain open and must agree with one another before the life cycle is considered complete.

## Communities and later arrivals

Social learning, household care, cultivation and industrial practice develop within an already diversified genus. Modern languages belong to much shorter histories than the biological branches, and can spread across them through teaching, trade and migration.

The Thal refugees arrive from Erde only about five thousand years ago. Their migration introduces new communities to Dverghamar; it does not initiate native dwarven evolution or determine the age of the genus.

## Habitats and arrival conditions

The earliest natural shelters occur in carbonate karst, old lava tubes and fractures beside productive habitats. Later mining and construction extend residence into competent intrusive and metamorphic massifs. Geology, water supply and food access must coincide; strong rock does not automatically contain natural large caves.

The preferred Thal founding history includes compatible Erde-derived cultivation and finite establishment provisions. Five millennia do not by themselves establish lifelong reproductive viability at Dverghamar’s gravity. [Thal refuge life](thals.md) describes the moderate-elevation settlement and provisioning conditions.
