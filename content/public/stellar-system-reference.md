All original project IP remains the author’s. Public documentation grants no license or right to reuse this work. Observations, cultural accounts, hypotheses and unresolved details retain their separate status.

## Local suns

Erde, Merenval and Dverghamar each have their own local sun; Merenval’s inhabited companion shares its sun. Distant suns can appear as conspicuous lights without providing familiar daytime warmth.

## Different experiences of light

Dverghamar’s ordinary landscape is organized around permanent sunward, nightward and twilight regions. Merenval and its companion have long days and slowly changing tidal conditions. The companion is a striking presence in Merenval’s sky, and Merenval in its companion’s.

## Observation and calendars

People can observe the sky, navigate, count time and develop local calendars without possessing the authorial model of the system. Mechanical clocks, remembered cycles and local conventions remain meaningful even where sunrise and sunset do not structure daily life. This guide gives no orbital parameter table or calculated stellar history.
