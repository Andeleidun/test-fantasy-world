# Familiars and shapeshifting

## A mutual bond

A familiar is an animal joined to a practitioner by a durable, mutual magical pathway. Affection and cooperation can support the relationship, but an ordinary animal companion does not become a familiar through closeness alone.

The animal must already be magically attuned. Attunement varies between individuals, even within one species; most animals remain oblivious to magic. The sapient participant needs at least latent sensitivity, with no single people holding exclusive eligibility.

A bond can develop gradually, form through a shared ritual or open during a crisis. None of these paths automatically produces the strongest or safest relationship. Mutuality remains essential.

## Two participants

Each participant has only one active familiar bond at a time. Loss or severance can leave a lasting psychological and magical scar. A later bond is possible, but does not erase the earlier relationship.

The practitioner and familiar retain separate minds. Sensations, emotions, instincts and perception can pass strongly through the connection, while the familiar continues to act through its own body. Bonding does not turn the animal into a tool or make it the automatic pilot of a transformed practitioner.

Attuned animals can have very particular abilities: an individual cat may find hidden Otherworld paths, a dog may follow otherwise impossible traces, or a raven may open an emergency passage. These examples do not make the abilities universal to their species.

## Learning a form

Shapeshifting uses the familiar’s **individual living pattern**, rather than a generic species template. Partial transformation is more accessible than a complete change. A practitioner may first acquire altered senses, balance, skin, claws or movement.

Complete transformation changes the practitioner’s own body. Differences in mass are handled through pattern-bound displacement and proportional magical cost. Greater differences demand more power, shorten safe duration and increase the danger of exhaustion or disrupted reversion.

Body, senses and compatible native magic can become accessible through a trained active bond. Memories, conceptual knowledge and learned skills do not transfer automatically. The familiar’s magic also requires sufficient training and a transformed body through which it can operate.

## Mastered forms and endurance

A genuinely mastered complete form becomes a permanent personal ability. The familiar’s death or the end of the conduit does not erase it. The retained form is an internalized pattern, not the familiar’s continued presence or a trapped soul.

Multiple forms can accumulate through successive relationships. Each brings associated instincts and habits of attention, creating interference and identity strain. The resulting forms become living parts of the practitioner: they age, heal and adapt with that person, rather than remaining snapshots or mirroring the former familiar at a distance.

The familiar’s native magic is not automatically retained after the bond ends. Independent retention or redevelopment of those powers remains unspecified.

Ordinary transformation is endurance-limited. A few masters can sustain a form indefinitely under favorable conditions while supplying magic and maintaining coherence. Starvation, injury, sleep, illness, emotional strain or interruption can still threaten the change. No form removes underlying aging or mortality.

## Moving between forms

Most practitioners organize partial or complete changes around one coherent template. A few masters can temporarily blend traits from several mastered forms, at added cost and risk. Physical traits combine more readily than native magical abilities.

Passing through the baseline body is the ordinary route between complete forms. Exceptional practitioners may shift directly, while the baseline remains the organizing reference. Composite forms and direct transitions increase the difficulty of keeping a stable sense of self.

## Repair and the living self-pattern

Transformation can genuinely repair injury. Magic guides the reconstruction, while blood, water, stored energy, proteins, minerals and existing tissue supply its lasting material. Repeated transformations cannot produce free regeneration.

Minor wounds are the reliable range for trained practitioners. Severe injury is more often stabilized than erased. Rare masters can regenerate a lost limb or similarly complex structure, but only with exceptional control, substantial bodily reserves and prolonged recovery. Ended living coherence cannot be restored by this process.

Repair follows the practitioner’s **living self-pattern**, not a universal ideal body. Longstanding anatomy, disability, scars, adaptations and embodied modifications may form part of that pattern. It changes slowly through lived experience, and deliberate retraining combines intention, ritual and sustained bodily practice.

Internal threats require recognition. Biological signals, sensory evidence, medical knowledge and magical training can help identify disease, toxins or parasites. An unfamiliar threat may survive a transformation even when its symptoms improve. Belief alone cannot make a mistaken diagnosis biologically true.

## Clothing, devices and gestation

Limited, deliberately prepared gear can accompany a transformation. The object must be physically present; preparation does not allow remote retrieval, duplication or unlimited storage. Unprepared clothing and equipment may fall away, tear or obstruct the change.

A deeply embodied prosthesis or implant can transform according to its integrated function. Mere ownership or surgical placement is insufficient. The treatment of wear, damage and maintenance across forms remains incompletely specified.

During pregnancy, parent and developing life can undergo a coordinated transformation that preserves their gestational relationship. They remain distinct living patterns. Temporary anatomy does not automatically rewrite the child’s heredity or grant the familiar’s species or memories.

Risk rises with gestational stage, incompatible anatomy, exhaustion, large size differences and complex transitions. Birth in a transformed state, long-duration transformed pregnancy and some equipment failure cases remain open. Carried children, riders, parasites and other nearby beings are not included simply through physical proximity.
