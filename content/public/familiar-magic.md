# Familiars and shapeshifting in practice

## A mutual relationship

Practitioners distinguish a familiar bond from ordinary affection for an animal. They experience a durable magical connection, shared sensations or emotions, and cooperation with a companion that retains its own mind and behavior. Individual animals differ; no species supplies the same ability in every member.

The relationship can grow gradually, form through ritual or emerge in a crisis. Familiar practice recognizes one active bond at a time. Loss or severance can leave lasting grief and magical difficulty; a later bond does not erase that history.

## Learning to change

Partial changes are more accessible than complete transformation. Altered senses, balance, skin, claws or movement may come first. Complete forms require sustained training. The learner does not simply acquire the companion’s memories or learned expertise.

Large differences in size and complex transitions are more exhausting and harder to sustain. Practitioners can experience fatigue, injury, disorientation or difficulty returning. These are practical observations, not an explanation of how matter or identity is maintained.

## Mastery and endurance

A mastered form can remain usable after a familiar is lost. Some practitioners learn further forms through later relationships, but competing instincts and habits of attention can become difficult. Returning through the ordinary body is the usual practice; direct changes and blended forms are exceptional.

Even accomplished practitioners face hunger, illness, injury, sleep and emotional strain. Stories of masters sustaining a form for extraordinary periods do not make that a reliable ordinary skill.

## Injury and recovery

Transformation can help with some injuries, but recovery still costs bodily strength and time. Severe wounds are more often stabilized than erased. A familiar-looking symptom may have an unfamiliar cause, and improvement does not establish a correct diagnosis.

Longstanding bodily features and scars need not disappear. Practitioners cannot promise one universal ideal body through transformation.

## Clothing and equipment

Some deliberately prepared belongings can accompany a change. Unprepared clothing and equipment can tear, fall away or obstruct movement. The ordinary guide does not provide technical explanations or guarantees for implants, gestation or complex equipment.
