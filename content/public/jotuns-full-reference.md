# Jotun societies and their reputations

All original project IP remains the author’s. Public documentation grants no license or right to reuse this work. Observations, cultural accounts, hypotheses and unresolved details retain their separate status.

## Mountain belonging

Jotun societies inhabit productive mountain margins and valleys, with demanding journeys into higher country. The selected public direction combines imposing cold-adapted bodies, distinctive protective features and sophisticated environmental knowledge. Detailed anatomy and ancestry remain unresolved.

## Authority and difference

Knowledgeable hosts, scholars, negotiators and communities valuing intellectual authority coexist with recorded coercive and violent clans. Some clans have long conflicts with dwarven neighbors. Neither bodily form nor the history of one clan fixes the morality of all Jotun.

## Atrocity and exaggeration

Accounts of especially cruel clans include captives displayed as trophies and status gained through conspicuous violence. These are accounts of atrocity, sometimes enlarged in retelling; they do not establish a universal custom or extraordinary biological immunity. Tales of thirty-foot giants likewise require distinction from ordinary descriptions.

## Open details

Local institutions, life histories and detailed regional variations await further description. Proposed aquatic or heat-associated branches are not introduced here as established peoples.
