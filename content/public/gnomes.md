# Gnomes

## A diverse people

Gnomes are small sapient people, commonly around three feet tall, with bodies built from differentiated fungal or fungus-like fibers, cords and membranes. The Gnome peoples vary greatly in color, texture, climate tolerance, body form, house tradition, language and government. Naturalists group these varied peoples within a broad related lineage.

## Body and medicine

Gnome anatomy is organized differently from animal-bodied peoples. Branching tissues carry nourishment, sensation and movement through a body capable of sealing, rerouting and regrowing after many injuries. Fire, drying, poison, infection, parasites and uncontrolled growth can still kill them. Some elders undergo marked mineralization.

Several traditions practice dethreading, in which a skilled Gnome loosens the assembled body into connected fibers, travels through a compatible living or porous substrate and re-forms. Solid stone is not automatically passable, and rescue medicine exists for failed passages.

## Living houses and kinship

Gnome homes are cultivated from many different organisms and symbiotic communities. A house may be a single organism, a colony, a multispecies composite or living tissue carried by wood, stone, crystal, ceramic or metal. Forest, wetland, desert, coast, cavern, alpine, urban and migratory peoples grow very different homes.

Children are often raised alongside a childhood house. Adults founding a household may bring living cultures from earlier homes into a new one. Gnome languages commonly distinguish bodily ancestry, upbringing, house descent, adoption and ancestral relationship, though no one grammar or naming system is universal.

## Houses, ancestors and awakening

Gnome practitioners distinguish a house’s living spirit, a household’s accumulated character, and the individual dead who remain in relationship with the family. Ancestral places reached through rite, dream or trained spiritual practice are not the same as bodily rooms grown into the Otherworld.

Some ancient houses appear to become individual persons. Scholars and religions disagree over how awakening should be recognized. Residents may worship diseased tissue as a mind or cut apart an emerging person as property. An awakened house may defend itself or move using defensive chemistry, constricting passages, subterranean cords, symbionts, detachable growths and Otherworld routes.

## Otherworld cultivation

Many Gnome traditions grow exploratory house roots into nearby Otherworld relationships. Successful roots may thicken into living passages, gardens, reservoirs or inhabitable chambers. Species differ in their needs and behavior; fusion, rejection, symbiosis, disease, predation and competition all occur. These living chambers are distinct from the ancestral places sought in spiritual practice.

## Craft and society

Assay gardens use changes in color, odor, light, growth or fruiting to warn of toxins, bad air, mineral seepage, disease or structural danger. The same attention to interdependent parts supports sophisticated clockwork, regulators, safety interlocks, replaceable modules and machines joined to living or chemical sensors.

Some Gnome governments sharply separate household and state. Others do not: a great living house may also be family, district, infrastructure, archive, temple, citizen and sovereign. Across these differences, a recurring political question is who controls continuity and when a relationship may rightly be broken.

## A city that is one house

One Gnome settlement has grown across centuries into a city-scale continuous house with its own personhood. Its courtyards, towers, tunnels, bridges, workshops, parks and neighborhoods are parts of one ancient lineage. Its roots and inhabited spaces extend into the Otherworld in ways no ordinary map can contain. Visitors, including Gnomes from elsewhere, regularly find it astounding; many residents do as well.

## Neighbors

Merenval’s living companion is a major Gnome world. Deep/Star Elves and Gnomes have long histories of contact in shared cavern regions without becoming one culture. Deep/Star traditions often approach crystal and cavern through resonance; Gnome traditions often approach the same places through metabolism, substrate, cultivation and succession.
