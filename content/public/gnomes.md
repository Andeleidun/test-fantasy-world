# Gnomes

## A fungal-looking people

Gnomes are small sapient inhabitants of Merenval's companion, commonly near three feet tall and strongly fungal in appearance. A genuinely fungal ancestry is the preferred direction, but their anatomy is still being designed. Movement, senses, breathing, circulation, support and thought will all need specialized living tissues.

Their colors and surface textures vary widely. Appearance does not determine family, work or temperament.

## Living homes

Gnome communities cultivate fungal dwellings in caves, sinkholes, humid pillar habitats and geothermal districts. The structures require food, support, ventilation, water management and continual care.

Families may join compatible living panels or keep different growths in adjoining spaces. Households often bring together six to ten relatives and partners in varied arrangements. Special edible growths can be cultivated for feasts without treating the whole dwelling as food.

## Gardens and long lives

Some settlements grow chemical alarms and defensive aerosols. Their effects remain local and depend on dose, wind and the creature exposed; they are not invisible walls that defeat every attacker.

A lifespan approaching five centuries is a design target, not yet a completed biological result. Late-life mineralization may become an aging or funerary process, but Gnomes do not automatically turn into living statues.

Deep Gnome settlements have long histories of exchange with Deep/Star Elves. Some settlements are mixed while the peoples remain biologically and culturally distinct.

Exact ancestry, anatomy, reproduction, growth and mineralization remain open.
