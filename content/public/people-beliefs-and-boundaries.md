## Public office and religious claims

Amadeus Cordelien holds a high-priestly or episcopal position within the king’s castle and advises the king. His public preaching associates concentrated spiritual and material authority with prosperity, security, salvation, and eternal life. These are descriptions of his office and teaching, not confirmation that the promises are true. The kingdom’s detailed legal and ecclesiastical institutions remain unresolved.

## Belief and interpretation

A creation story, religious tradition or sincerely held account can matter culturally without establishing what happened. Core sapient peoples have diverse traditions. Elven reputations for magical knowledge do not make every Elven claim authoritative.

## The Howling Void in warnings and belief

The name Howling Void appears in terrifying accounts of destruction and dangerous boundaries. Fragmentary reports and prohibitions do not establish a reliable art of controlling what they describe. Stories about it should not be used to explain every disappearance, unfamiliar creature or failed spiritual encounter. Its true nature is outside this public account.
