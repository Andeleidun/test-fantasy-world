# Public persons, beliefs, and dangerous boundaries

## Public office and religious claims

Amadeus Cordelien holds a high-priestly or episcopal position within the king’s castle and advises the king. His public preaching associates concentrated spiritual and material authority with prosperity, security, salvation, and eternal life. His promises have not been verified. The kingdom’s detailed legal and ecclesiastical institutions remain unresolved.

## Belief and interpretation

The peoples of the inhabited worlds tell different creation stories and hold different religious beliefs. Their traditions often disagree. Even Elves, widely respected for magical knowledge, can be mistaken about origins or spiritual encounters.

## The Howling Void in warnings and belief

The name Howling Void appears in terrifying accounts of destruction and dangerous boundaries. The reports are fragmentary, and no reliable means of controlling it is known to ordinary practitioners. The reports offer no dependable explanation for other disappearances, unfamiliar creatures or failed spiritual encounters. Its nature remains unknown to them.
