# Elven peoples and traditions

> Historical research snapshot, retained through 14 September 2026. For the current public edition, use [the public collection](public/README.md). This file is not a current publication source. Prose edited on 20 September 2026; historical decisions and data retain their earlier status.

## One people across two worlds

Elves originated on Merenval and remain one fully interfertile, geographically structured population. Their history includes four major traditions: Forest Sylvan, Maritime/Littoral Sylvan, Crystalline/High and Deep/Star. These traditions are cultural and ecological histories, not species, castes or biologically fixed temperaments.

Several migration waves founded the companion population around 84,000–82,500 years before present. Later contacts, including distinct pulses around 43,000–41,000, 19,000–18,000 and 5,000–4,000 BP, kept ancestry braided across the pair. Rare background migration continued between larger events.

## Four traditions, three approaches

Forest and Maritime/Littoral Sylvans share a broad **reciprocity** approach to magic. Forest practice emphasizes growth, succession and negotiated relationships among living systems. Maritime practice emphasizes flow, coasts, currents and the overlapping cycles of sea and land.

Crystalline/High practice emphasizes **topology, structure and complexity**. Crystal arrays are instruments and built systems rather than proof that the companion is made from giant crystals.

Deep/Star practice emphasizes **resonance and hidden nodes**. Its strongest traditions developed among deep-cave populations of the companion, where routes, echoes and distant relationships could be studied over long timescales.

Schools borrow from one another, and individual practitioners often disagree.

## Sylvan diversity

Forest Sylvans inhabit Merenval's forests, wetlands, river systems and cultivated living landscapes. Maritime/Littoral Sylvans form an interlinked network across coasts, islands and estuaries. Their coherent identity develops around 38,000–32,000 BP through voyaging, exchange and intermarriage rather than a single founder event.

Maritime populations may show modest diving and marine-foraging adaptations. They do not possess gills, breathe seawater or drink salt water without physiological cost. Cultural belonging does not require a particular ancestry profile.

## Companion traditions

Crystalline/High communities are often associated with monumental structures, resonant arrays and exacting topological arts. A taste for order, fractal design or serenity appears in communities with different governments and among people with different personalities.

Deep/Star communities form a persistent companion metapopulation by roughly 55,000 BP. They have long histories of contact with Gnome settlements in fungal, cavern and geothermal environments. Exchange and mixed settlements do not erase either people.

## Wayroots and Dorrenath

Dorrenath is the eldest known Wayroot, a city-scale magical organism and genealogical elder on Merenval. It is not the creator of all life, a world tree holding up reality or a cosmic axis. Wayroots can establish unusually stable overlap with the Otherworld, but not every tree develops a passage and no route is automatically safe.

## Quantitative targets and open questions

The working demographic model uses a mean parental generation interval near 150 Earth years, with 120–180 years as its main sensitivity range. Differentiation values in the Merenval reference are simulation targets rather than measurements or species boundaries.

Final lifespans, maturity schedules, physiology, present population sizes, local political histories, native-language names and the frequency of modern interworld travel remain open.
