# Merenval and its living companion

## A binary pair of inhabited worlds

Merenval is a near-Earth-gravity terrestrial world orbiting the K-type member of the selected wide hierarchical triple. Its inhabited companion is about one quarter of Earth's mass and follows a close mutual orbit. The barycenter lies far outside Merenval, making the pair an unequal binary terrestrial system even when inhabitants call the smaller world a moon.

The current working values are about 1.05 Earth masses and 1.04 Earth radii for Merenval, and 0.25 Earth masses, 4,380 km radius, 0.53 g and 6.7 km/s escape velocity for the companion. Their separation is near 375,000 km and their mutual period near 23.2 Earth days.

## Complementary climates

Merenval is the wetter world, with roughly 68% ocean and a 1.4-bar nitrogen-dominated atmosphere. Its broadly connected oceans, forests, wetlands, archipelagos and river networks buffer its long days.

The companion is about 68% land and 32% ocean, with a working pressure near 1.75 bar and the same target oxygen partial pressure of about 0.21 bar. Deep separated seas, immense continents, high plateaus, rain-shadow interiors and monsoonal margins make it more climatically continental. Low gravity and dense air strongly favor flight and gliding.

Both worlds are intended to rotate near, but not exactly at, their 23.2-day mutual orbital rate. Merenval's illustrative 21.5-day spin would make the companion circuit its sky in roughly 300 days. Atmospheric thermal tides are the leading balancing mechanism, but the paired spin equilibria and orbital migration remain unvalidated.

## One tree of life, two planetary persons

The worlds share one ancient tree of life, preferably through impact-ejecta transfer early in their history. The original world of abiogenesis remains open. Billions of years of mostly separate evolution allow strong ecological divergence despite homologous biochemistry.

Both biospheres are deep and persistent enough for distinct mature planetary spirits under the current cosmology. Neither spirit is subordinate to the other, and proximity does not merge them into one person.

## Peoples and landscapes

Elves originated on Merenval in the current working history. Sylvan populations remain centered there, including Forest and Maritime/Littoral traditions. A deliberate multi-wave colonization of the companion around 84,000–82,500 years before present founded the ancestry shared by the companion's Crystalline/High and Deep/Star populations.

The companion also sustains major Gnome populations in cave, fungal, sinkhole and humid pillar habitats, and major Orc populations across savannas, canyonlands, dry plateaus, inland-sea margins and river valleys.

Dorrenath, the eldest known Wayroot, is a city-scale living structure on Merenval. Wayroots can develop unusually stable overlap with the Otherworld, though not every tree produces a usable passage. Dorrenath is a genealogical and ecological elder, not a creator tree or cosmic axis.
