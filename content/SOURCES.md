- **Mountain Giants**: approved Jotun direction, provisional size and gravity constraints, cultural inspiration and explicit ancestry/physiology gates.
- **Gnomes** and **Orcs**: current biological and cultural outlines. Fungal ancestry and long life remain targets for Gnomes; Wild Orc travel bias is not genus-wide moral biology.

This synchronization does not select an exact Merenval spin equilibrium, companion tide model, final Gnome/Orc/Jotun physiology or any unresolved regional ecology.

## System A chronology correction — 14 September 2026

The Drive authority records were corrected and read back before this repository synchronization: the [current decision baseline](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1NITmWlwB_aCnI6evA7UAhPPkerVvTb7mAgczTwn5swg/edit), [Dverghamar canon](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1mtjV9MC9EatFItPRmlZqZTLMARmWcXqcWraacAiFgpI/edit), [evolutionary synthesis](https://docs.google.com/document/d/11dQvRDaZ4fIizEpDtax6ezazSza9uW1F5VjdyK2kmns/edit), [open questions](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1gXKXI1YBDDppp3CCgMc4njSnldMi4bgIGD7PCJTSNWw/edit), [scientific review](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1KOAiA8JMpKVBnDSLZjOAnRGJiExcskbuQfJ3UsOG6LY/edit) and [Merenval baseline](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1TG6w5DamlzUL-wuGL9S6PsZLeP0OdUARYAz2ReZBqMY/edit).

System A's shared age is settled at approximately 4.54 billion years. Erde's geological chronology is favored and retained. Dverghamar's former 7–9-billion-year and 8-billion-year scaffolds are superseded; its adapted reference now uses a 0.50-solar-mass slow-rotating early-M host, about 0.0379 solar luminosities, 1.20 Earth flux at 0.1776 AU and a 38.65-day synchronous orbit. Its revised biological milestones are about 3.70 Ga for established microbial life, 2.70 Ga for sustained oxygenic photosynthesis, 1.25 Ga for macroscopic aquatic ecosystems and 0.80 Ga for the six-limbed radiation. The recent dwarf lineage and later Thal arrival remain unchanged. Scientific validation of the adapted model remains open, but the chronology choice does not.
