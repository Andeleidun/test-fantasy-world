## A realm with its own conditions

The Otherworld is part of the developed cosmological framework. Crossings can connect material places, including the route through which the Thals reached Dverghamar. Its topology, hazards, time and native ecologies require their own rules.

Its routes should not be read as ordinary tunnels through planetary rock. Environmental conditions and practitioner-dependent protection shape what passage permits and what it costs.

## Living spirits and emergent souls

The current framework distinguishes living spirits from souls. Soulhood involves developed personal continuity; it is not assigned identically to every living thing. Exceptional collectives can develop agency and, under the selected conditions, personhood.

Planetary spirits arise from living worlds. A planetary spirit need not already be a planetary person capable of personal consent. That distinction matters when worlds are cultivated, preserved or integrated.

## Integration and consent

When both planetary spirits possess collective souls, both planetary persons must consent to integration. Living inhabitants also materially influence the outcome through their relationships, actions, resistance and survival, even though unanimous population consent is not a universal metaphysical gate.

A pre-personal host cannot give personal consent. Its distributed compatibility or resistance must instead be interpreted through converging ecological, geological, magical and spiritual responses. No single sign is decisive.

## Preservation has a continuing cost

A preservation network depends on ongoing contributions. Every contributing soul may withdraw immediately. Remaining capacity determines whether the network redistributes the burden, weakens or fails.

A mature network maintains reserve capacity, overlapping contributors and trained replacements. Staged degradation protects essential functions before peripheral ones, while temporary emergency contributions can stabilize the network without requiring permanent commitments.

## A separate boundary

The Howling Void lies outside coherent reality and is not the organizing cause of ordinary planetary life. It should not turn unrelated geology, cultures, spirits or histories into disguised expressions of one cosmic threat.
