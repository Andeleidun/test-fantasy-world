The new script records running/failed/completed state separately from scientific acceptance so interrupted runs cannot be presented as completed validation.

## Limits to the acceptance tests

- The margin-envelope width, 75% target-fit heuristic and 30 km residual-gap screen are authorial design criteria, not published scientific thresholds.
- Gateway substrate dates and vertical histories are chosen hypotheses. Checking that their inequalities hold does not independently establish those dates or elevations.
- High/low coastline tests are static endpoint configurations. Intervening shelf geometries and actual sea-level chronology are not supplied.
- The 200 km-radius nucleus parcels occupy a small fraction of the planet's crust. Their non-overlap and area conservation cannot be generalized to the external margins.
- Minimum gap distance can use different shores on one connected land component. It is not a complete terrestrial travel, navigation or founder-demography calculation.
- Permanent water at two selected island sites does not audit every surviving, extinct or freshwater lineage.
- All reported geographical changes concern an authorial candidate. No naming proposal or revised atlas is silently promoted to public canon.
