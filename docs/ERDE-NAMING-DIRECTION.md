# Erde: identity and naming direction

## Established direction

Erde is the primary world of its inhabitants. They call it Erde. Its peoples, societies, countries and histories arise within that world; Earth geography is an authorial reference, not their identity or their past.

Public articles, maps, captions, search results and introductions present Erde directly. Do not introduce it as rotated Earth, a familiar planet with a changed axis, or a setting populated by equivalents of present-day Earth cultures. How the world was constructed belongs in authorial documentation unless a later publication explicitly chooses to discuss it.

Earth-reference coordinates, archaeological analogues and geographic correspondences remain useful in authorial files. Preserve those mappings during renaming so that changes in terminology do not silently move a population or change its history. Scientific uncertainty remains visible in public prose where it changes what a reader can infer, without explaining the construction process.

## Naming layers

1. **Planet:** Erde is established. “People of Erde” is an available collective description; it includes more than one ancestry and species.
2. **Continents and major lands:** develop widely used map names, then regional endonyms and exonyms as languages and contact histories become clearer. A map name is not necessarily universal native usage.
3. **Geographic demonyms:** identify origin, residence or association with a land. They do not establish a race, species, unified nation or language.
4. **Cultural people-names:** belong to particular communities and histories, potentially spanning continents and biological lineages.
5. **Biological lineage names:** describe descent. They need not be the names living communities use for themselves.

Do not mechanically import the seven-continent convention, Earth north/south labels, current nations or modern ethnic categories. Continental boundaries and directions must be checked against Erde’s geography. Until proper names are selected, use clear descriptive wording tied to known habitats and routes.

## First naming slate: proposals, not canon

The following names are new project proposals. No historical etymology, real-world uniqueness, native language or universal acceptance is asserted. The demonyms are possible forms for the reader-facing common register. Six reference areas organize this first comparison; the count is not yet a settled continental taxonomy.

| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Africa | Oratha | oh-RAH-tha | Orathan |
| Connected Europe–Asia landmass | Veyra | VAY-ra | Veyran |
| North America | Tavren | TAV-ren | Tavreni |
| South America | Selvara | sel-VAH-ra | Selvaran |
| Australia–New Guinea / Sahul region | Maruun | mah-ROON | Maruuni |
| Antarctica reference landmass | Ilyra | ih-LEE-ra | Ilyran |

Europe and Asia need not be separate continents in Erde’s geographic tradition. Australia and New Guinea need not be continuously connected at the atlas epoch merely because their reference shelf is grouped here. The reference North/South American labels do not establish Erde compass directions. Decide geographic extents before assigning formal continental borders.

**Erden** (ER-den) is a proposed concise adjective for something or someone of Erde. “The peoples of Erde” remains preferable when a collective noun might imply one species or civilization. The planet name itself does not imply all its languages share German grammar or vocabulary.

## Inhabitants: next naming work

Retain **Thals**. Human and hobbit are presently readable broad descriptions; they need not import Earth's exact biological species. Replace Neanderthal, Denisovan, erectus, sapiens and island-derived taxon labels in the public narrative with established native names or accurate descriptive lineage wording until their names are chosen.

The five regional complexes currently labeled Boreal Rim, Great Watersheds, Volcanic Hinge, Highland Spine and Equatorial Basin are not five continent-wide ethnicities. Keep useful descriptive labels provisionally, and replace misleading compass/climate labels with accurate descriptions. In particular, “Boreal” must not imply the climate of every population in the former Boreal Rim.

Give individual peoples their own names after identifying which community, language, epoch and self-understanding the name belongs to. Do not assign a demonym to everyone descended from an ancient regional founder regardless of later migration or identity.

## Migration and review

- Public descriptions use the established setting directly; proposed proper names stay in this authorial document until selected.
- Public atlas variants remove Earth comparison panels, reference-location labels and authorial development notes. Preserve original atlas sheets in the authorial source set.
- Source crosswalks remain discoverable for maintenance; ordinary article introductions do not tell the making-of story.
- Tests check the public Erde articles, map text and generated reader for Earth reference labels and unapproved proposal names. Public scientific units elsewhere are a separate editorial question and must not be silently recalibrated to Erde.
- Once names are selected, update articles, map labels, dictionary references, search data and internal crosswalks together. Do not change stable page IDs solely to rename a continent.
