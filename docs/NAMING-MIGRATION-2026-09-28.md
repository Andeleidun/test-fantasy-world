# Hamarkar / Dverghamar naming migration — 28 September 2026

## Final canonical assignment

This record documents the final accepted naming architecture and supersedes earlier project material where these names were assigned differently.

- **Hamarkar** is the planet/world.
- **Hamarkaran** is the neutral adjective or demonym for origin on Hamarkar.
- **Dverghamar** is the broad collective ethnonym for the indigenous dwarven peoples of Hamarkar. In reader-facing English, **dwarf/dwarves** remains the ordinary translation/exonym.
- **Dverghamar language family** is the broad reference label for the dwarven language family.
- **Korvar** remains the shared interregional standard.
- **Rakkor/Rakkorar** and **Menkor/Menkorar** remain narrower identities whose exact assignments are still open.
- Modern **kor** means **person** and is productively available in appropriate people/member terms.

## Superseded assignments

The project passed through two earlier naming states.

The original architecture used **Dverghamar** for the world and **Hamarkor/Hamarkorar** for the general dwarven people-name. A later same-day migration improved the place/people distinction by assigning **Hamarkor** to the world and **Dverghamar** to the people. That intermediate assignment is also superseded.

The following uses are therefore not current canon:

- **Dverghamar** as the planetary name.
- **Dvergahamrar** as an archaic or formal planetary name.
- **Hamarkor** as the planetary name.
- **Hamarkor/Hamarkorar** as the general dwarven ethnonym.
- **Hamarkorvar** as the language-family name.
- **Hamkor/Hamkorar** as a proposed contraction of the former general ethnonym.

Historical documents and source snapshots may preserve these forms when their dated wording is useful provenance. They do not override the current assignment.

## Why Hamarkar replaced Hamarkor

The intermediate planetary name **Hamarkor** preserved the desired sound and stone association, but it conflicted visibly with the productive modern morpheme **kor**, “person.” Treating the planetary final -kor as historically unrelated was linguistically possible but required readers to ignore the most obvious analysis every time they encountered the productive element elsewhere.

**Hamarkar** removes that collision while preserving the established phonetic and aesthetic identity of the name.

The resulting system is deliberately clearer:

- **Hamarkar** = world.
- **Dverghamar** = broad indigenous dwarven people-name.
- **kor** = person.
- **Rakkor**, **Menkor**, **Kelkor**, and future productive -kor forms can be interpreted consistently as person/member formations.

## Linguistic interpretation

**Hamarkar** is an inherited planetary proper name. Its first element remains historically associated with the **hamar** stone/crag lexical family. The final **-kar** belongs to an older toponymic stratum and has no selected productive modern meaning.

The deeper history of -kar is intentionally unresolved. It may eventually be reconstructed as descending from an old spatial, territorial, bounded-place, land, body, or domain term, but none of those meanings is presently canon. It must not simply be defined as modern “world” unless a later language-history decision establishes that derivation.

Modern **kor** remains unambiguously “person.”

**Dverghamar** functions as a fossilized collective ethnonym rather than a regular productive singular/plural form. The current Korvar reference therefore does not derive a plural by simply adding ordinary **-ar** to Dverghamar.

## Usage

Use **Hamarkar** for the planet in narration, maps, diplomacy, scientific discussion, and ordinary reference.

Use **Hamarkaran** for planetary origin or association:

- Hamarkaran geology
- Hamarkaran gravity
- Hamarkaran wildlife
- a Hamarkaran mineral
- a Hamarkaran person, regardless of whether that person is Dverghamar, Thal, Jotun, or another people

Use **Dverghamar** for the indigenous dwarven peoples collectively and attributively:

- Dverghamar engineering
- Dverghamar settlements
- Dverghamar languages
- the Dverghamar peoples

Use **dwarf/dwarves** where ordinary reader-facing English is preferable.

## Public compatibility policy

The public reader keeps established route IDs, source IDs, anchors, and asset filenames such as `dverghamar.html` and `dverghamar-*.svg` where changing them would break existing links or provenance. These are compatibility identifiers, not current display names.

Reader-visible titles and prose use **Hamarkar** for the world and **Dverghamar** for the people.

The reader build contains an ordered compatibility migration for preserved source snapshots. Those snapshots predate the naming changes and may contain both **Dverghamar** as the world and **Hamarkor/Hamarkorar** as the people. The build converts those roles separately so regeneration cannot reintroduce either superseded assignment.

## Authority

The current Drive authority chain records the decision in the project baseline and canon register, with the detailed planetary and language consequences carried by the Hamarkar scientific and open-question records.

Where a dated source snapshot or earlier migration note conflicts with those current records, the latest accepted Hamarkar/Dverghamar assignment governs.
