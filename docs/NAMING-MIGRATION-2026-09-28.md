# Hamarkor / Dverghamar naming migration — 28 September 2026

## Canonical assignment

This record documents the accepted naming migration and supersedes earlier project material where the two names were assigned differently.

- **Hamarkor** is the planet/world.
- **Hamarkoran** is the neutral adjective or demonym for origin on Hamarkor.
- **Dverghamar** is the broad collective ethnonym for the indigenous dwarven peoples of Hamarkor. In reader-facing English, **dwarf/dwarves** remains the ordinary translation/exonym.
- **Dverghamar language family** is the broad reference label for the dwarven language family.
- **Korvar** remains the shared interregional standard.
- **Rakkor/Rakkorar** and **Menkor/Menkorar** remain narrower identities whose exact assignments are still open.

## Superseded uses

The following uses are no longer current canon:

- **Dverghamar** as the planetary name.
- **Dvergahamrar** as an archaic or formal planetary name.
- **Hamarkor/Hamarkorar** as the general dwarven ethnonym.
- **Hamarkorvar** as the language-family name.
- **Hamkor/Hamkorar** as a proposed contraction of the former general ethnonym.

Historical documents and source snapshots may preserve these forms when their dated wording is itself useful provenance. They do not override the current assignment.

## Linguistic interpretation

Both names are treated as old, partly fossilized forms rather than transparent modern compounds.

**Hamarkor** retains a visible historical association with the stone/crag lexical family through *hamar*, but the complete etymology remains unresolved. Modern **kor**, “person,” must not be redefined as “world” to explain the planetary name.

**Dverghamar** likewise functions as a fossilized collective ethnonym rather than a regular productive singular/plural form. The current Korvar reference therefore does not derive a plural by adding ordinary **-ar** to Dverghamar.

## Public compatibility policy

The public reader keeps established route IDs, source IDs, anchors and asset filenames such as `dverghamar.html` and `dverghamar-*.svg` where changing them would break existing links or provenance. These are compatibility identifiers, not current display names.

Reader-visible titles and prose use **Hamarkor** for the world and **Dverghamar** for the people. The reader build contains an ordered compatibility migration so preserved source snapshots cannot reintroduce the superseded assignment during regeneration.

## Authority

The current Drive authority chain records the decision in the project baseline and canon register, with the detailed planetary and language consequences carried by the Hamarkor scientific/open-question records. Where a dated source snapshot conflicts with those current records, the current accepted decision governs.
