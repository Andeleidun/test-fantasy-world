# Dorrenath: nearer mountains and current public description

23 September 2026. Targeted update following the author's acceptance of a clear crown-above-mountains silhouette.

## Decisions and limits

- Dorrenath's selected real-space height remains approximately 25 km above local ground. She remains one living person, the eldest known Wayroot and Great Mother in the sense described by her inhabitants.
- The author accepted the visual relationship of her broad crown passing cleanly above substantial nearby mountains. A lower rocky shoulder on either side can approach her crown and, where branches clear the slope, reach beneath its edge. Taller mountains chiefly stand farther back. The trunk stays on a broad low terrace with flanking valleys and an open route to the southern gulf.
- A crown approximately 150 km wide, particular ridge distances and elevations, the exact gulf site, color and species of upper foliage, and snowfields remain design proposals. The revised illustration is a scenic concept, not a measured terrain or branch-clearance model.
- The full authorial Elves, Merenval, planetary, and physical-scaling records were updated in their existing source documents. This public edition confines itself to observable landscapes, broadly known relationships, and attributed beliefs. It does not publish the detailed metaphysical or structural mechanisms developed in private authorial notes.

## Later age and underground-water decision

The author subsequently selected a working age of roughly one to two million Erden years for Dorrenath as one continuous living individual. The author also selected a vast replenished underground water system with a true lake contacted by her roots. The public Merenval and Elves sources now describe that lake and acknowledge her history without giving public observers a verified numeric date or treating the present branches and waterways as unchanged since her origin. Its aquifer extent, recharge rate, lake geometry, and living water partners remain unresolved. The latter are explored as proposals in the private regional and metaphysical design notes; they have not been converted into public species descriptions.
